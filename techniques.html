Manufacturing Techniques

Every rug, made-up, and architectural floor covering manufactured at Real Carpets India is defined by specialized structural engineering. From generational hand-knotted heirlooms and high-density tufted wool to flatwoven pit-loom kilims and chunky hand-knits, explore our complete manufacturing repertoire.

Panipat Industrial Repertoire

9 Structural Constructions

Direct ownership of pit-looms, tufting tables, and finishing yards allows us to engineer floor coverings tailored to your exact target FOB prices, yarn blends, and pile heights.

Cut & Loop Pile

Hand-Tufted

Artisan-guided tufting guns shoot yarn through a stretched cotton primary backing. Allows for intricate multi-colour organic curves, deep sculpted high-low reliefs, and luxurious plush pile heights.

Pile Height 10 mm to 25 mm
Fiber Pairing New Zealand Wool, Viscose, Bikaner Blend
Backing Compound 100% Cotton Canvas + Natural Latex
Best Applications Living Room Area Rugs, Hospitality Suites

Reversible Flatweave

Hand-Woven (Pit-Loom)

Flatwoven interlocking warp and weft crafted on generational pit-looms by master Panipat weavers. Fully reversible, highly durable, easy to clean, and naturally textural for casual modern living.

Structure 100% Reversible Flatweave
Fiber Pairing Combed Cotton, Pure Wool, Jute Blends
Weight 1400 - 2200 GSM
Best Applications Scandi Runners, Casual Living, Dining Rugs

Heirloom Investment

Hand-Knotted

The pinnacle of rug craftsmanship. Each knot is hand-tied row by row over vertical loom warps. Produces unmatched longevity, exceptional knot density, and heirloom-grade investment carpets.

Knot Density 30 to 120 Knots / Sq. Inch
Knot Structure Tibetan & Persian Asymmetric Knots
Fiber Pairing Hand-Spun NZ Wool, Silk & Bamboo Blend
Best Applications Flagship Residential, Luxury Penthouses
View Matching Rugs →
Artisanal Tapestry

Panja / Kilim

Traditional heavy iron claw (panja) beats weft yarns tightly over cotton warp cords. Creates authentic geometric motifs, crisp line work, and dense, compact architectural tapestries.

Tooling Heavy Iron Panja (Claw) Tool
Fiber Pairing High-Twist Wool, Recycled Cotton Warp
Finish Fringe Hemming, Tassel Knots
Best Applications Tribal Rugs, Wall Hangings, Floor Mats
View Matching Rugs →
Chunky Soft Textile

Hand-Knitted

Heavy-gauge artisan hand-knitting creating chunky cable-knit poufs, structural floor cushions, and tactile throws. Hand-finished with reinforced hidden zippers and durable inner lining.

Gauge Giant Cable & Rib Knit
Core Filling EPS Beads or Dense Foam Block Core
Fiber Pairing Cotton Cord, Merino Blend, Polyester Core
Best Applications Floor Poufs, Throws, Accent Soft Furnishings

Coiled Natural Fiber

Braided

Continuous braided coils of natural golden jute, cotton cord, or recycled chindi fibres sewn together in concentric spirals. Creates organic circular and oval mats, planters, and storage caddies.

Construction Concentric Spiral Interlocking Braid
Fiber Pairing Tossa Jute, Bleached Cotton, Upcycled Yarn
Reversibility 100% Dual-Sided Usable Surface
Best Applications Round Rugs, Oval Runners, Storage Baskets
View Braided Items →
Dense Micro Loop

Hand-Hooked

Uncut loop pile technique pulling yarn loops through backing using an artisan hooked needle tool. Produces firm, dense, pebble-textured surface aesthetics that resist tracking and high foot traffic.

Pile Structure Continuous Tight Loop Pile
Texture Pebble Grain, High Foot-Traffic Resilience
Fiber Pairing 100% Wool, Cotton Canvas Base
Best Applications Kitchen Runners, Entryway Mats, Accent Rugs
View Hooked Rugs →
