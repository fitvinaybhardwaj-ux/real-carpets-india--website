Panipat Industrial Unit

Our Infrastructure

Our manufacturing unit spreads over an extensive industrial area in Panipat, Haryana. All core departments operate synchronously under one roof.

Administrative Division

Dedicated international client servicing, CAD designing, and freight clearance desks.

Weaving & Tufting Sheds

Generous natural-light workshops housing pit-looms, panja frames, and tufting gantries.

Yarn & Material Godowns

Moisture-controlled warehousing for pure wool, combed cotton, and raw jute fibre.

Artisan Living Quarters

Dignified on-site housing ensuring artisan welfare and fair social labour compliance.

Quality Laboratory

In-house testing equipment for colourfastness, tensile strength, and AQL inspections.

Container Stuffing Bay

Direct loading bays equipped with moisture desiccants and weather-sealed baling equipment.
