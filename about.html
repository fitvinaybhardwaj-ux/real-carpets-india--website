Our Heritage & Manufacturing Prowess

Founded as an artisan workshop over 30 years ago, Real Carpets India has grown into a premier Govt. Recognized Export House. By uniting traditional Indian craftsmanship with direct spinning mill partnerships, in-house dyeing vats, tufting gantries, and a rigorous 17-stage technical manufacturing process, we deliver end-to-end reliability for overseas home furnishing brands.

Our Proven Heritage

Built around rugs. Developed for global retail.

Real Carpets India started its journey in 1995 as a trusted supplier of hand-tufted carpets to leading export houses around Panipat, Haryana.

By delivering uncompromised quality, zero-defect weaving integrity, and absolute punctuality, we built the operational muscle and technical expertise required to supply top-tier international retailers directly.

Today, Real Carpets India operates an extensive manufacturing setup in Panipat, mastering Hand-Tufted, Hand-Woven, Hand-Knitted, Machine-Tufted, Braided, and Hand-Knotted constructions. Our design and merchandising teams collaborate intimately with overseas home brands, department stores, and hospitality procurement desks to turn design visions into certified container deliveries.

Our Vision

To be the most reliable, technically advanced, and ethically compliant manufacturing partner for floor coverings and soft home furnishings worldwide.

Our Mission

Bridging generational Indian artisan weaving techniques with international sustainability, non-toxic dyes, and stringent ISO/AQL quality thresholds.

Our Manufacturing Facilities

Administrative Division
