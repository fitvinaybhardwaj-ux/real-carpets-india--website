Our Proven Heritage

Built around rugs. Developed for global markets.

Real Carpets India started its journey in 1995 as a supplier of hand-tufted carpets to leading exporters around Panipat, Haryana.

By producing consistent, high-integrity products with an uncompromising 0% rejection track record, we built the technical foundation and operational confidence required to serve overseas customers directly.

Today, Real Carpets India operates an extensive manufacturing setup in Panipat, mastering Hand-Tufted, Hand-Woven, Hand-Knitted, Machine-Tufted, and Braided constructions. Our dedicated design and merchandising teams collaborate intimately with overseas home brands, wholesalers, and procurement teams to transform design concepts into market-ready floor coverings.

Zero Rejection Heritage

Integrated Quality Systems

Quality is not treated as a final afterthought; it is structurally integrated into each production phase through standardized ISO 9001:2015 operating procedures.

1
AQL Level II Statistical Sampling:

Defect classification mapping strict dimensional tolerances, stitch density, and colour conformity.

2
100% Calibrated Daylight Inspection:

Every single rug and bathmat is unrolled under overhead high-CRI daylight lamps to audit surface shearing.

3
Industrial Conveyor Metal Detection:

All finished goods pass through electronic metal detector gates to guarantee zero broken needle or wire fragments.
