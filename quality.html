Standardized Quality Laboratory

Unlike small decentralized workshops, Real Carpets India maintains an on-site testing facility evaluating yarn tensile strength, rubbing colourfastness, and AQL 2.5/4.0 defect tolerances prior to bale packing.

Every carpet undergoes 4 separate physical gate audits: after tufting, post-latex curing, shearing, and final packing.
